This project in partnership with the Estonian Civil Protection and Rescue Board (Päästeamet), is aimed at designing and developing accessible browser-based interactive scenarios to simulate emergency situations (extreme weather events, energy shortages, situations of conflict, etc.) to help Estonian citizens being prepared in case of crises. It is one applied project developed through the broader [ARDIN Design Studio initiative](/projects/ardin-design-studio/).

Team members: Ahmed Pasha Faisal
