This project in partnership with the Estonian Civil Protection and Rescue Board (Päästeamet), is aimed at designing and developing accessible browser-based interactive scenarios to simulate emergency situations (extreme weather events, energy shortages, situations of conflict, etc.) to help Estonian citizens being prepared in case of crises.

Team members: Ahmed Pasha Faisal


See more on the project in the [ARDIN Design Studio Website]('https://designstudio.ardin.online/portfolio/interactive-simulatios-crisis-preparedness/')
