This supervised Erasmus+ traineeship project explored an accessible tool for navigating scholarship on interactive digital narratives. The project brought together one bachelor's student and one master's student from Palacký University Olomouc to investigate how open-access large language models and knowledge graphs could support the discovery and exploration of research literature.

Interns: Anežka Formánková and Anna Kovaříková.
