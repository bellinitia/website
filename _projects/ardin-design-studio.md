ARDIN Design Studio is an ASTRA Knowledge Transfer project at the University of Tartu focused on establishing a consultancy for the design and development of prosocial interactive narratives.

As project manager, I lead the initiative with supervised employee Ahmed Pasha Faisal. The Studio's collaboration with the Estonian Civil Protection and Rescue Board (Päästeamet) includes the applied [Interactive Simulations for Crisis Preparedness](/projects/interactive-simulations-crisis-preparedness/) project.
