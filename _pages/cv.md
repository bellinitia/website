Work experience
======
* September 2025: <b>Postdoctoral Researcher</b>
  * University of Technology of Compiègne, Sorbonne University, France
  * Duties includes: Narrative and experience design of immersive mixed reality experiences for the representation of life in the internment and deportation camp of Compiègne-Royallieu. Evaluation and testing of the interactive scenarios.
  * Supervisor: Prof. Serge Bouchardon

* November 2025: <b>Knowledge Transfer Project Manager</b>
  * University of Tartu, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Institute of Cultural Research
  * Duties includes: Managing two knowledge transfer projects: "Interactive Narrative Design for Representing Complexity" and "Making Research Accessible through Generative AI" (ASTRA Project PER ASPERA).
  * Supervisee: Ahmed Pasha Faisal,  Dr. William Ruddock Primett

* November 2024 - September 2025: <b>Research Assistant</b>
  * University of Tartu, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Institute of Cultural Research
  * Duties includes: Assisting in research activities within the Institute of Cultural Research, drafting and submitting funding applications.
  * Supervisor: Prof. Marina Grishakova

* October 2022 - August 2024: <b>Junior Research Fellow in Comparative Literature</b>
  * University of Tartu, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Institute of Cultural Research
  * Duties includes: Conducting research on interactive digital narratives and complexity representation, teaching courses such as "Introduction to Cognitive Humanities" and "Narrative Studies".
  * Supervisor: Prof. Marina Grishakova

* September 2018 - June 2019: <b>Team Leader, Web Design and Development</b>
  * EIT Services, Camorino, Switzerland
  * Duties includes: Leading the web design and development team.
